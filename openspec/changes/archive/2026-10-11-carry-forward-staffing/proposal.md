## Why

For a project Savills already runs, the Head of PM's instruction is: copy last year's staff roster into
the new budget, recalculate it, and when a line goes up or down, say why in the remark. The tool does
the opposite today. It treats every project as a new prospect and estimates the roster from the average
of 88 other buildings. On a low-rise project that is already managed (called "project X" below) the
back-test of 2026-10-10 showed the tool inventing 20 management positions and overshooting the staff
cost line (EMH2) by about 1.8 times the real 2027 line (the tool came to about 2.8 times it). The real file did what the Head of PM describes: EMH2 carried forward from the
approved 2026 budget with a one-sentence reason for the change.

## What Changes

- Add a **project mode** on `Project Info`: "New prospect" (today's behaviour, unchanged) or "Existing
  project".
- In Existing-project mode, add a **Prior-Year Roster** block to the input workbook: position, shift,
  headcount, monthly gross salary per head (VND), and which approved budget year it comes from. The roster
  is the headcount and salary baseline. Source priority for a position becomes: prior-year approved roster,
  then headcount the PM typed, then the ratio estimate (the ratio estimate stays the fallback and its rows
  are labelled as estimated).
- **Recalculate** the baseline for the budget year under Vietnamese state law only: regional minimum wage,
  the insurable-salary base and ceiling, and the employer contribution rates. These come from a new
  **labour-law parameter table** with effective dates and the legal source of every row, not from numbers
  written into the code. No percentage uplift is invented by the tool.
- Recalculate the **night-shift and public-holiday allowance** (EMH3) from the calendar of the budget year
  and generate its reason automatically (for example one more public holiday).
- Every staffing line that differs from the baseline (the rows of `A1. Staff cost`, EMH2, EMH3, EMH4) must
  carry a **reason**: either generated by the tool (law or calendar) or chosen by the PM from an approved
  list of Vietnamese reason codes plus a note. **Verification fails** when a changed line has no reason.
- The remark on those lines keeps the **guideline's original remark first**, then appends a Vietnamese
  sentence "Điều chỉnh so với NS <năm>: …" stating old figure, new figure, difference in VND and the reason.
- Add an internal **Staff bridge** sheet: prior year, then wage and insurance effect, headcount effect and
  allowance effect, then this year. It ties exactly to EMH2 and EMH3 and is not on the client deliverable.
- Provide an **optional field and hook** for actual headcount from C&B payroll and PM timesheet data, shown
  beside the baseline when present. The integration with those data sets is not built here.

Not in this change: management-fee rate, electricity, outsourced service contracts (security, cleaning,
landscape) and any other rate. They are separate later changes. Existing behaviour that stays: a zero typed on a
ratio-covered row is still not a way to force the role to zero; the row is deleted instead (see
`portfolio-derived-staffing-defaults`).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `existing-project-staffing`: existing-project mode, prior-year roster baseline, labour-law recalculation,
  allowance recalculation, reason rule, Staff bridge, optional actual-headcount field.

### Modified Capabilities

- `portfolio-derived-staffing-defaults`: in Existing-project mode the prior-year roster outranks the ratio
  estimate; New-prospect mode is unchanged.
- `deliverable-remarks`: the adjustment sentence follows the guideline's original remark on changed staffing lines.
- `budget-workflow-verification`: verification fails on a changed staffing line without a reason, and checks that
  the Staff bridge ties to EMH2 and EMH3.
- `input-workbook-reading`: the generators read the project mode and the Prior-Year Roster block by label.

## Impact

- **Reads (new):** the labour-law parameter table (one CSV under `data/generation_B_verified/`), the input
  workbook's new fields, `vn_holidays.py`. Reads the optional actual-headcount field when it is filled.
- **Writes (new):** a Staff bridge sheet in `Project_Budget_Output.xlsx` (internal only). The client deliverable
  gets only changed remarks on staffing lines in Existing-project mode.
- **Code touched at apply time:** `build_project_input_form.py`, `generate_project_budget.py` (staffing demand
  and `A1`/`EMH2`/`EMH3`/`EMH4` filling), `generate_project_output.py` (payroll constants now hard-coded as
  `STATUTORY_*_2026`), `input_labels.py`, `verification/checks.py`.
- **Recorded baseline figures:** none move in New-prospect mode (before/after measured on the standard sample,
  expected zero cell differences). In Existing-project mode the figures are new by definition; the measurement
  is the acceptance test in `design.md` and `tasks.md`.
- **Sign-off:** the Head of PM approves the reason-code list and the acceptance result before archive.
- **Data protection:** real project names and real payroll never go into this change, its tests or the repository;
  test data is invented.
